4. NEURAL FALLBACK TRANSLATION & TTS ENGINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To ensure uninterrupted audio delivery even during upstream network fluctuations
or translation cluster degradation, a resilient fallback engine was added:

  * File: server/src/tts-fallback.js
  * Architecture:
    - Intercepts error events with code 'translation_error' or missing downlinks.
    - Dispatches rapid neural text translation via high-availability translation
      gateways to listener target languages.
    - Connects to Ollalink's dedicated /v1/tts/speak WebSocket stream.
    - Synthesizes 48kHz mono s16le PCM voice using native models (nh-m01 for
      Hindi, nh-ot03 for English).
    - Emits both final caption events and real-time audio chunk frames directly
      to the recipient peer WebSocket.

5. FORENSIC AUDIO RECEIVING AUDIT & ROOT CAUSE FIXES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A deep inspection of the audio receiving pipeline across Rust, JavaScript, and
WebSockets uncovered three distinct flaws that led to audio lag, stutter, and
desynchronization:

Flaw A: Dual Binary & Base64 Ingest Pollution (Primary Desync Cause)
  * Root Cause:
    In server/src/server.js and dist-package/server/src/server.js, fallback chunks
    were transmitted with both 'audio_b64' inside the JSON metadata text frame AND
    an immediate binary buffer frame.
    In app/src-tauri/src/ws/mod.rs, when 'audio_b64' was present in the JSON frame,
    the receiver decoded it and called 'continue', skipping the insertion of an
    entry into expected_audio_queue.
    When the binary frame arrived immediately following the text frame,
    expected_audio_queue had no metadata entry. The binary frame was classified
    as an orphan and appended to pending_binary_queue.
    Every fallback chunk created an un-paired orphan. On subsequent utterances or
    normal audio streams, the receiver popped the stale orphan from the previous
    sentence instead of pairing the current chunk, resulting in playback delay,
    out-of-order audio, and severe stutter.
  * Solution:
    1. Removed 'audio_b64' from server-side fallback audio transmissions in both
       server/src/server.js and dist-package/server/src/server.js. Audio is now
       sent purely as binary PCM frames matching standard Ollalink protocol.
    2. In app/src-tauri/src/ws/mod.rs, if both 'audio_b64' and binary frames are
       received simultaneously, an ExpectedChunk with sample_rate 0 is enqueued,
       instructing the binary handler to cleanly discard the redundant binary frame.

Flaw B: Utterance Boundary Queue Leakage
  * Root Cause:
    When an utterance ended ('is_marker' or 'is_last'), only expected_audio_queue
    was cleared, leaving any orphaned frames inside pending_binary_queue to leak
    into the next sentence.
  * Solution:
    Updated ws/mod.rs to clear both expected_audio_queue AND pending_binary_queue
    on true utterance boundaries, ensuring total acoustic isolation between sentences.

Flaw C: Jitter Buffer Contention & Channel Safety
  * Root Cause:
    In app/src-tauri/src/audio/jitter.rs, flush_resamplers() iterated sample-by-sample
    under mutex lock, causing lock contention during playback flushes. In addition,
    fill_into() lacked a lower-bound clamp on out_channels.
  * Solution:
    1. Applied .max(1) to out_channels to prevent zero-stride callback loops.
    2. Replaced sample-by-sample loops with ring.drain(0..to_remove) and bulk
       ring.extend() slice operations.
